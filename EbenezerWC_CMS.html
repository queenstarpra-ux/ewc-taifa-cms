ℹ️ How to set up

1. Go to Configure accounts above

2. Enter the church Mobile Money numbers for each network

3. Enter the account name (name registered on the MoMo)

4. QR codes are automatically generated for each account

5. Members scan the QR code to initiate payment directly

6. After paying, they click Confirm payment made to record in the system
